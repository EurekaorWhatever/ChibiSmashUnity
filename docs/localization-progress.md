## Notes for later stages

- Fragments: where code builds a sentence from pieces ("Signed in" + " as " + name) the pieces were first converted to
  whole `{0}` patterns where practical; any left as fragments are listed here for stage 2.
- The globe button is drawn by NavBar(), so it is absent on pages without the top bar (character select, camera
  check), where the side panels fill the top corners.

## Left for the lead / later stages (2026-11-24)
